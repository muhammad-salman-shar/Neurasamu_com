NeuraSaMu exists to close the distance between thought and action. Founded in September 2023 by Muhammad Salman Shar, the laboratory studies continuous cognition — systems that infer, consolidate, and plan beneath the threshold of interaction — and engineers it into physical automation and beneficial technology for human life. Its corpus spans two architectures: continuous subconscious processing and a hierarchical memory graph for LLMs.

Keywords — artificial general intelligence · superintelligence · embodied robotics · continuous inference · synthetic cognition

02Vision

محمد سلمان شر Muhammad Salman Shar — Founder & Chief Executive Officer

The inception

In September 2023, Muhammad Salman Shar — the youngest founder and computer-science student becomes first AGI researcher in Pakistan — established NeuraSaMu as the country's first frontier laboratory for artificial general intelligence, superintelligence, and robotics.

Before the lab, the discipline had no address in Pakistan. Now it does.

The conviction

Theory that never touches the physical world is decoration. Every architecture we study is engineered toward utility — automation delivered, decisions improved, human effort returned.

And the capability to build it belongs here: sovereign models, sovereign tools, sovereign talent.
