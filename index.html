AbstractNSM — 01
Frontier Laboratory
Karachi → Lahore → World

NeuraSaMu exists to close the distance between thought and action. Founded in September 2023 by Muhammad Salman Shar, the laboratory studies continuous cognition — systems that infer, consolidate, and plan beneath the threshold of interaction — and engineers it into physical automation and beneficial technology for human life. Its corpus spans continuous subconscious processing, a hierarchical memory graph for LLMs, an Anthropomorphic Breach Hypothesis for AGI containment, an evidence-weighted outlook on AGI and ASI through 2031, and a local-first neuro-symbolic cognitive architecture (NSM VB).

Keywords — artificial general intelligence · superintelligence · embodied robotics · continuous inference · synthetic cognition
